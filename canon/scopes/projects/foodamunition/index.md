# Food&Ammo project description and goals

Food&Ammo aims to build supply chains and logistics in the physical world so that
vital food, medicines, essential stocks and equipment reach where they are needed.
Current development combines supply-chain knowledge, information about resources
and participants, and tools for accountable coordination. Its public website is [foodamunition.group](https://foodamunition.group).

The present description defines the intended product. It does not claim an
operating procurement or delivery network, verified suppliers, emergency
response, a complete knowledge base or measured revenue.

## Intended product

| Area | Intended contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Map and shelters | Information about relevant places and resources, keeping object existence, permission to publish and current availability separate. |
| Supply and contractors | A way for logistics providers, manufacturers, trade networks and other solution providers to contribute capabilities and develop useful supply arrangements. |
| Wiki and Heritage Vault | Traceable knowledge and reference material, with source rights and suitability for reuse made explicit. |
| I'm Alive | Consented status information. A missing report remains an unknown state; it does not establish a person's safety, injury or death. |
| Approval Programme | A proposed private quality-assessment process with explicit criteria and evidence. Payment is not proof of quality, and a private mark is not official authorization. |
| Social and community | Participation, contribution and accountable handling of feedback and disputes. |

These areas are a product direction, not a list of available services. Each
capability needs its own evidence and applicable authorization before a public
claim can describe it as operating.

## Initial objectives and acceptance evidence

| Objective | Evidence required before claiming the result |
| --- | --- |
| Explain the project truthfully | The published discovery pages and supported languages state the actual project stage, preserve the mission and offer clear, working next steps. Verification names the version served to visitors. |
| Make useful contributions accountable | A controlled check demonstrates consent handling, persistence, notification and review acknowledgement separately. An accepted proposal records the responsible reviewer and decision; a successful form response alone is insufficient. |
| Build a reusable knowledge and object example | A bounded example preserves provenance, rights, uncertainty and independent review. A synthetic example stays labelled synthetic; candidate knowledge stays distinct from admitted knowledge and from verified real-world capability. |
| Verify a useful provider or resource relationship | Current evidence supports the specific capability and its scope. Any proposed arrangement has an accountable owner and follows the existing authorization path before a real-world effect. |
| Test economic sustainability | A defined user need, proposed service, cost basis and evidence of willingness to use or pay support a commercial hypothesis. Actual income and margin require records from an identified source. |

The initial objectives are intended to produce useful, checkable progress. They
do not assign unmeasured numerical baselines, invent customers or make a sales
forecast. A solution-provider service and reuse of reviewed knowledge are income
hypotheses to investigate, not established revenue streams.

## Evidence and current delivery state

The project is in development. Record each capability's planned scope,
implemented version, verified behavior and authorization separately. A prototype
or source change does not establish an available service; a successful deployment
does not establish fulfilment, responsible handling or income.

Evidence records identify what was checked, the relevant version, the observation
date, the responsible owner and remaining limitations. Claims about an object,
provider or person's status must be updated or withdrawn when their evidence
expires or no longer supports them. Unknown information remains unknown.

Product milestones are reviewed against these objectives and the
[space goals](../../spaces/foodamunition/index.md). New evidence may change the
next priority. A change to the founding mission or commercial commitment requires
an explicit decision and a reviewed successor description.

## Scope and ownership boundaries

The owner space is `foodamunition`. The workspace registry retains current
membership and repository locations. Existing knowledge owners retain native
Knowledge Contract meaning and admission. Existing systems retain task state,
identity, permissions and release responsibility.

Private knowledge, operational records, personal information, locations without
publication permission and credentials are excluded from this public
description. External commitments and physical work require their existing
accountable authorization; this document grants none.

This draft transfers no writer, issuer, identity, promotion, publication or
runtime authority. It is not an admitted native project Charter, an AuthoringGrant
or a compiled Canon release. Parent bindings and native admission must be
supplied and verified through their existing routes. `runtime_eligible` remains
`false`.
