# Food&Ammo space description and goals

Food&Ammo is an Arcanada project space whose mission is to build supply chains
and logistics in the physical world so that vital food, medicines, essential
stocks and equipment reach the people and places that need them. Current
development produces knowledge, software and coordination tools toward that
mission. Physical actions follow their existing accountable authorization.

This is a descriptive source candidate. It records the intended purpose and
division of responsibilities; it does not establish an operating delivery service
or activate a space, policy or agent.

## Goals

| Goal | Evidence of progress |
| --- | --- |
| Useful knowledge for essential supply chains | Traceable knowledge with reviewed sources, a defined use and acceptance through the existing knowledge process. An editorial candidate and admitted knowledge remain distinct. |
| Reliable information about places, resources and providers | Separately supported statements about existence, permission to publish and current availability or capability, each with its own scope and observation date. |
| Accountable participation | A contribution can be received, assigned to a responsible reviewer and followed through to an evidenced decision. Submission, notification, acknowledgement and qualification remain separate events. |
| Clear public information | Published pages describe the actual maturity of each capability and provide useful next steps. Their claims are checked against the version visitors receive. |
| Sustainable development | Proposed services and knowledge reuse are assessed for user value, cost and a plausible income path. Revenue and margin are reported only from an identified accounting source. |

These goals do not assert that the corresponding outcomes have already been
achieved. A missing baseline is recorded as not measured, not as zero. Counts of
documents, messages, tasks, tests or releases are activity measures; they do not
by themselves establish useful knowledge, fulfilled supply or income.

## Project and shared work

The [Food&Ammo project description](../../projects/foodamunition/index.md) defines
the intended product and its initial evidence requirements. Food&Ammo can reuse
the Arcanada ecosystem's knowledge, coordination and engineering services without
replacing their owners or contracts.

Food&Ammo and Initiative 10 are distinct projects. They may reuse research and
architecture, while Initiative 10 retains responsibility for its own problem
analysis. Shared work does not merge their missions or create a new business
project. Current membership and repository locations remain defined by the
existing workspace registry; this candidate does not alter them.

## Responsibility and iteration

Product and research work defines the goal, proposed change and evidence needed.
Engineering implements a bounded change and returns the exact version and its
verification results. Independent review checks that version against the agreed
criteria. Cross-project dependencies are resolved with their existing owners.

Each iteration records the goal it serves, starting evidence, useful result,
remaining uncertainty and the owner of the next concrete step. It also records
an error or learning, the improvement applied, and whether this description needs
to change. Repeating an unresolved blocker without new evidence is not recorded
as progress toward a product outcome; the next iteration must reconsider the
dependency, decomposition or route to its owner.

The description is reviewed when the founder clarifies the mission, evidence
changes a material product claim, responsibility changes, an assumption fails,
or repeated work stops advancing a goal. A successor preserves the reason for
the change and its source, receives independent review and keeps links to the
previous version. A new commercial hypothesis or a change to the mission remains
an explicit decision, not an accidental consequence of implementation.

## Scope and authority boundaries

Planned capability, implemented source, verified behavior and authorization are
reported separately. Evidence for one does not establish the others. Source
publication is distinct from a compiled, admitted Canon release.

Existing owners retain knowledge admission, task state, identity, permissions,
deployment and external-action authority. Consent and publication rights remain
required for person-related information. A source description is not authority
to purchase, contract, publish a private location or perform physical work.

This draft transfers no writer, issuer, identity, promotion, publication or
runtime authority. It is not an admitted native space Charter, an AuthoringGrant
or a compiled Canon release. Private operational policies, personal records and
credentials remain with their authorized owners. `runtime_eligible` remains
`false`.
